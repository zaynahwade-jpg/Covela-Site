Our mission

Covela is a student-created platform built to strengthen science learning through peer support, clear explanations, and collaborative exploration. Our mission is to connect tutors and learners within schools while giving students everywhere a space to deepen their understanding, share knowledge, and grow together — all through a modern, accessible, student‑driven approach to science.
