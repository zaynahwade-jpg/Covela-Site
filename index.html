Our mission

Covela is a student-created platform designed to connect science tutors and learners within schools. While Covela can be easily implemented into individual school tutoring programs, it still allows students everywhere to use its tools to explore and master science topics collaboratively.

Why Covela?

Collaborative Learning

Study groups and tutoring sessions designed to make science engaging and social.

Progress Tracking

Dashboards and KPIs help you measure your growth and celebrate milestones.

Resources Hub

Access curated materials, labs, and study guides all in one place.
